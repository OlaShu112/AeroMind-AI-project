# Introduction to Philosophy

Philosophy is the systematic study of fundamental
questions concerning existence, knowledge, reasoning,
values and reality.

## Major Branches

### Metaphysics
Studies the nature of reality and existence.

### Epistemology
Studies knowledge, belief and justification.

### Ethics
Studies morality, values and human conduct.

### Logic
Studies principles of valid reasoning.

### Political Philosophy
Studies justice, authority, rights and government.

## Philosophical Inquiry
Philosophy uses critical thinking, logical argument,
conceptual analysis and questioning to investigate
fundamental problems.
